Hi %contact_first_name%,

Quick one.

When I closed on my own first home in Altamonte Springs in January 2024, the part of the contract that gave me the most peace of mind was not the financing contingency or the appraisal. It was the inspection period.

On the AS-IS version of the standard Florida purchase contract (the form most Orlando resale deals use), the inspection period is the one contingency that lets you walk away for almost any reason and keep your deposit.

It's not just for when the inspector finds something. It's your window to decide whether everything about the place actually works for you: the home itself, the HOA documents, the commute you tested at 8am, the noise at night.

The other contingencies are narrower. Appraisal protects you if the home appraises below the contract price. Financing protects you if your loan falls through. Both useful, but specific.

The inspection period is the broad one. Use it that way.
