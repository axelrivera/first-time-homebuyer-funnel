**Subject:** The lender fear that costs you real money

Hey {{first_name}},

Most first-time buyers do not shop lenders.

The reason they give: "I don't want to take a hit on my credit score from multiple inquiries."

That fear is costing them, on a typical Orlando starter loan, easily $25,000 to $55,000 over the life of the mortgage. Here's why.

The credit bureaus treat multiple mortgage inquiries as a single inquiry, as long as they fall inside a rate-shopping window. For most scoring models the window is about 14 days. Get all your hard pulls done inside two weeks and your score moves the same as if you had only talked to one lender.

So the actual rule is:

1. Pick 2-3 lenders before you talk to anyone. Your bank, one credit union, and one independent mortgage broker is a fine starting set.
2. Get rate quotes and Loan Estimates from all of them inside the same two-week window.
3. Compare apples to apples. Same loan type, same down payment, same closing date.
4. Pick the best Loan Estimate.

Quote spreads of 0.25-0.5% between lenders are normal. On a $450K loan, 0.25% is roughly $75/month and about $27,000 over 30 years. Worth two weeks of focused work.

One thing to watch. A "Loan Estimate" is a specific federal document the lender has to give you within 3 business days of your full application. If a lender quotes you a rate verbally but won't put a Loan Estimate in writing, that's not a real quote. Treat verbal rate quotes the way you'd treat a verbal job offer.
