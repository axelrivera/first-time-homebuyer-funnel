Hey *|FIRST_NAME|*,

Quick story about how I ended up in Altamonte Springs.

I moved to Orlando from Puerto Rico in 2017, after Hurricane Maria. I rented a small apartment in Downtown Orlando and worked as a software consultant. For three years, Downtown Orlando was where I lived, worked, and knew people.

In May 2020, during COVID, I started cycling. My apartment was right next to the Cady Way Trail, so I could ride straight from my door. That convenience built the habit. By the end of that year, cycling was the thing I planned my week around.

When I started looking at homes in 2024, trail access was at the top of my list. The Downtown Orlando neighborhoods that had it were priced well above what I wanted to spend. Altamonte Springs covered most of my checklist, including the item that mattered most, at a better price. Less than a mile from the Seminole Wekiva Trail.

Before cycling, I never would have considered Altamonte. The only thing I knew about it was the Apple Store at the mall.

That's the thing I now help buyers figure out on purpose: knowing what shapes your daily life lets you see areas you'd otherwise skip. The roadmap can list neighborhoods. It can't tell you that Altamonte has a trail running through it that changes everything. You only see that if you know what you actually care about.

If you've got an area on your shortlist (or one you've already dismissed), reply with the name. I'll send my honest read.

-- Axel
