Quick check-in. You're a few weeks into having the roadmap. Hope it's clarifying more than it's complicating.

One Florida-specific thing worth getting on your radar now, even if you're months from closing: the homestead exemption.

What it is: Florida lets you reduce your home's assessed value by up to $50,000 for property tax purposes on a primary residence. On a $450K house, that's the difference between paying tax on $450K and paying tax on roughly $400K. Real money, every year you own the home.

There's a second piece called Save Our Homes that becomes just as valuable over time. Once you file homestead, the annual increase in your assessed value is capped at 3% or the rate of inflation, whichever is lower. In a normal appreciation environment, that cap saves owners thousands by year five and tens of thousands by year ten.

The catch: it's not automatic. You have to apply with the county property appraiser by March 1 of the year after you take title. Miss the deadline and you wait a full year to file again, eating the full tax bill in the meantime.

When you eventually close on a place, put it on your calendar. The application is short, free, and online for every county in the Orlando metro (Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake).
