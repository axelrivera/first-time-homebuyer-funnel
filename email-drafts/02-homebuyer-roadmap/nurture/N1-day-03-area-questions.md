**Subject:** The questions the roadmap doesn't ask

---

Hey {{first_name}},

Three questions to ask yourself before you tour anything in Orlando. The roadmap doesn't ask them. They're worth more than any individual step.

1. **Commute math.** How far are you willing to drive (or how often are you willing to be on I-4 at 8am)? Sanford and Apopka push price per square foot down because they push commute up. If your job is in Lake Mary, no cost. If it's Downtown Orlando, 45 minutes a day, every day.

2. **School-zoning resale floor.** Schools set the resale floor for the next buyer, even if you don't have kids. Seminole County zoning is uniformly strong across cities; Orange County zoning is uneven block to block. Same house, two different zoning realities, two different 5-year resale numbers.

3. **The 5-year plan.** Is this your home for 7+ years or 5-7? Below 5 years, closing costs and selling commissions usually eat any appreciation. A starter SFH in Sanford is great for 7+. A townhome closer to Downtown Orlando is great for 5-7. Different answers shift you to different areas.

If one of these is the question you're stuck on, reply with where you are and I'll send my quick version of how I'd think about it. No pressure on what you do with it.

-- Axel
