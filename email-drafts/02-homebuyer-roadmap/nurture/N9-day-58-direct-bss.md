# FTHB Roadmap - Day 58 - Direct BSS

**Subject:** Most first-time buyers wait too long to have this conversation

**Preview:** Early is when it's useful. Under contract is when most of it is already set.

**Send:** Day 58 (7 days after N8)

---

```
Hi %contact_first_name%,

The most common reason people put this conversation off: they think they need to be further along first.

It is backwards.

The decisions worth talking through get made early. Which areas give you the most house at your number. How long you plan to stay, and what that changes about where you buy. How offers actually get structured here.

By the time you are under contract, most of that is set. The conversation still helps then, but it turns into damage control instead of strategy.

You do not need a pre-approval or a target neighborhood to book. Questions are enough.

That is the last email in this sequence. 30 minutes, free, video or in person.

👉 [Book the Buyer Strategy Session]

Prefer to meet in person, or want to ask something first? Call or text %agent_mobile_phone% or reply to this email.
```
