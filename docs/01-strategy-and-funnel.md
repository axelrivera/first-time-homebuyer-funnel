
## The problem this funnel solves

A new real estate agent's biggest enemy is being treated as a generalist who "sells houses." First-time buyers in Orlando are paralyzed by uncertainty. Not lack of interest. They don't know *if* they're ready, *when* they'll be ready, or *what* the process actually looks like. They Google, they doom-scroll Zillow, and they wait. Most agents respond to this by handing out a "Buyer's Guide" PDF that no one reads, then chasing them with "just checking in" texts.

This funnel does the opposite. It gives the prospect **diagnostic clarity in 7 minutes** and earns the right to be the person they call when they're ready.

                                    │

│              │   │   Sprint       │   │   Phase          │

       │                    │                    │

│ CTA: Book    │    │ CTA: Get LM2   │   │ CTA: Nurture     │
│ Buyer        │    │ (Process Map)  │   │ email sequence;  │

The funnel does not ask everyone to book a call. It segments by readiness and offers the next-right thing for each tier. **This is the most important design choice in the funnel**, and it is what makes the Hormozi "make them feel stupid to say no" math work.

## Hormozi value equation applied

> Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)

### LM1: The Readiness Filter

| Driver | How LM1 maxes it out |
|---|---|
| **Dream Outcome** | Clarity about whether and when they can buy a home in Orlando. Not a house yet. Most first-time buyers at this stage are stuck on uncertainty, not on inventory. LM1 sells the resolution of that uncertainty. |
| **Perceived Likelihood** | 10 specific questions → a tier with a number → 2 specific mistakes → real Orlando price data. Feels diagnostic, not promotional. |
| **Time Delay** | 7 minutes to result. Inline. No "we'll email you in 24 hours." |
| **Effort & Sacrifice** | No math. No documents. No agent call. No commitment. Plain-English answers. |

### LM2: The Process Map

| Driver | How LM2 maxes it out |
|---|---|
| **Dream Outcome** | "Walk into the Orlando home-buying process knowing more than most buyers who've done this before." |
| **Perceived Likelihood** | Named, specific gotchas across the Orlando metro (HOA timelines, roof inspection in rainy season, Seminole vs. Orange school-zone resale math, the Sanford-to-Downtown-Orlando commute-and-price trade-off, the Lake Nona stretch math at $575–600K). Hyper-local proof of expertise. |
| **Time Delay** | Delivered immediately. Readable in 15–20 minutes. |
| **Effort & Sacrifice** | Visual map, not a wall of text. Anchor link per step so the reader can re-enter the document at the exact step they're on, without re-reading what they already know. |

## Why two steps and not one

A single-form "Book a Strategy Session" CTA only converts people already at the bottom of the funnel (Tier A). Everyone else (the much larger group) bounces.

LM1 captures the **entire intent spectrum** by giving every visitor a useful, segmented result. The 90-Day Sprint segment is the largest group and the one most agents fail at. LM2 was designed specifically to keep them warm and educated until they cross into Tier A. The Foundation Phase segment is a long-term play. Saved for nurture, not pitched.

This is Hormozi's "Give, Give, Give, Ask" content philosophy applied as a funnel architecture: the *Asks* are gated behind enough *Gives* that they don't feel like asks.

## Where each magnet is promoted (warm outreach map)

LM1 is the **top of every CTA the agent puts into the world**. Specifically:

- The link in every Instagram and Facebook bio
- The pinned post on each profile
- The "P.S." in every 1-on-1 warm outreach message: *"P.S. I built a quick 7-minute readiness scorecard if you ever want to see where you actually stand. Free. No call required. [link]"*
- The end-of-video CTA on every YouTube short and reel
- The link in the agent's email signature
- The QR code on any printed material (open house signs, business cards)

LM2 is **only promoted in two places**:

- As the next step inside the LM1 90-Day Sprint result
- As a standalone offer for content that specifically addresses the home-buying *process* (e.g., a post that breaks down a single step of the 9-step roadmap)

This keeps LM1 as the single dominant entry point and prevents the agent from fragmenting attention across too many lead magnets.

## The end goal: the Buyer Strategy Session (BSS)

Both magnets terminate at the same place: a **30-minute free BSS**.

The BSS itself is not a sales call. Per the Hormozi rule (*"making the customer feel like they're getting a deal, not being sold to"*), the call is structured as:

1. Recap the prospect's tier and scorecard answers (agent already knows them)
2. Walk through the 1–3 specific obstacles their score surfaced
3. Hand the prospect a written action plan they can use *with or without the agent*
4. End with: *"If you want me to walk this with you, here's how that works. If you want to do it on your own, you have everything you need. Either way works for me."*

The funnel exists to fill the BSS calendar with prospects who already have a tier, a timeline, and a list of obstacles named, so the agent walks into every call as a diagnostician, not a salesperson.

## What this strategy explicitly avoids

Lifted directly from the Hormozi rules in the project knowledge base. The funnel **never** uses any of the following:

- Paid ads or boosted social posts of any kind
- Cold outreach to people who do not know the agent
- Lead-list purchases, scraped contact databases, or skip-traced names
- Pop-up "Get my free home valuation" widgets
- "Just checking in" follow-up messages with no value attached
- Generic "First-Time Buyer's Guide" PDFs with no diagnostic specificity

If a tactic is not documented in *$100M Offers* or *$100M Leads*, it is not in this funnel.
